2026 PPC Bot Audit Checklist

27 field audit checkpoints across 4 core phases for detecting and neutralizing invalid traffic across Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, and analytics.

📅 Version 2026.1
📊 27 Checkpoints
⏱️ ~45 min audit
01

Google Ads & Performance Max Hygiene

7 checkpoints — Search Partner exclusions, Display Network placements, geo-location targeting vs intent settings, click threshold scripts

Exclude Search Partners at Campaign Level
Disable Search Partners for all campaigns unless explicitly tested. Partners often deliver 3–5× higher invalid click rates with zero conversion value.
Google Ads High Priority
Audit Display Network Placements Weekly
Review Placement Reports for parked domains, made-for-advertising (MFA) sites, and apps with >90% bounce rate. Add negative placements in bulk via Editor.
Google Ads High Priority
Verify Geo-Targeting: "Presence" vs "Interest"
Ensure campaigns use "Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations" — NOT "Presence or interest." Interest targeting opens floodgates to VPN/datacenter traffic.
Google Ads High Priority
Implement Click Threshold Scripts (GAS)
Deploy Google Ads Scripts that auto-exclude IPs exceeding configurable click thresholds (e.g., >5 clicks/hr, >15 clicks/day) with 24-hr lookback windows.
Google Ads Medium Priority
Audit Performance Max Asset Group Signals
Review audience signals for each asset group. Remove overly broad signals (e.g., "All users") that invite bot traffic. Replace with high-intent custom segments.
Performance Max Medium Priority
Enable Invalid Click Protection in Click Quality Settings
Confirm "Automatic filtering" is ON in Settings → Account Settings → Click Quality. Review the Invalid Clicks report weekly for manual exclusions.
Google Ads High Priority
Set Up IP Exclusion Lists (Shared Library)
Create shared IP exclusion lists for known VPN ranges, datacenter ASNs, and internal corporate IPs. Apply at account level via Shared Library → Audience Manager.
Google Ads Medium Priority
02

Meta Ads & TikTok Fraud Controls

7 checkpoints — Audience Network exclusions, lead ad honeypot questions, CAPI deduplication, short-session bounce triggers

Disable Audience Network for Conversion Campaigns
Turn off Audience Network placement for all conversion-optimized campaigns. AN delivers 40–60% bot traffic on lead gen and app install objectives.
Meta Ads High Priority
Add Honeypot Fields to Lead Forms
Include a hidden CSS-only field (display:none) in Instant Forms. Submissions with this field filled = bot. Filter via CAPI or CRM webhook.
Meta Ads High Priority
Enable CAPI Event Deduplication (event_id)
Pass unique event_id from browser pixel to server-side CAPI. Meta deduplicates within 48 hrs — prevents double-counting bot conversions that fire both client & server.
Meta Ads High Priority
Implement Short-Session Bounce Triggers
Fire a custom "Low Quality Visit" event when session duration < 3s AND no scroll/interaction. Use as negative audience in retargeting and conversion optimization.
Meta Ads Medium Priority
Disable TikTok Pangle Network for Non-App Campaigns
Pangle (TikTok's audience network) drives high-volume, low-quality traffic. Disable unless running app install campaigns with strict postback validation.
TikTok High Priority
Validate TikTok Click ID (ttclid) Deduplication
Ensure ttclid is captured on landing page and passed to server. Deduplicate server-side within 24 hrs — TikTok's attribution window allows replay attacks.
TikTok Medium Priority
Audit Creative-Level Click-Through Rates for Anomalies
Flag creatives with CTR > 3× account average AND conversion rate < 0.5%. Typical bot signature: high clicks, zero downstream events.
Meta Ads / TikTok Medium Priority
03

GA4 & Telemetry Bot Segmentation

7 checkpoints — Unassigned traffic spikes, 0-second engagement filters, direct/none anomaly detection, known datacenter ASN flags

Create "Unassigned Traffic" Alert (GA4)
Build Exploration: Session source/medium = "(not set)" OR "unassigned". Alert if > 5% of sessions or > 20% revenue unattributed. Indicates bot traffic stripping UTM params.
GA4 High Priority
Filter 0-Second Engagement Sessions
Create segment: engagement_time_msec = 0 AND event_count = 1 (page_view only). Exclude from all conversion audiences and lookalike seeds.
GA4 High Priority
Detect Direct/None Anomaly Spikes
Monitor direct/none traffic for sudden > 50% WoW increases with > 90% bounce. Correlate with paid campaign pauses — bots often hit direct when paid stops.
GA4 Medium Priority
Flag Known Datacenter ASNs (BigQuery Export)
Join GA4 BigQuery export with IPinfo/MaxMind ASN database. Flag sessions from ASNs: Amazon (16509), Google (15169), Microsoft (8075), DigitalOcean (14061), OVH (16276), Hetzner (24940).
GA4 / BigQuery High Priority
Implement Bot Probability Scoring (Custom Dimension)
Score each session 0–100 based on: 0s engagement (+30), datacenter IP (+25), no scroll (+15), single page (+10), known VPN (+20). Push to GA4 custom dimension for segmentation.
GA4 Medium Priority
Validate Enhanced Measurement Events
Confirm scroll, video_play, file_download, form_start events fire correctly. Bots rarely trigger scroll > 90% or video_play — use as negative signals.
GA4 Medium Priority
Set Up Real-Time Bot Dashboard (Looker Studio)
Build dashboard: Sessions by Bot Probability Score, Invalid Click Rate by Campaign, Datacenter ASN Top 10, Revenue at Risk. Schedule daily email to media buying team.
GA4 / Looker Studio Medium Priority
04

Clawback & Refund Playbook

6 checkpoints — Google Ads invalid clicks report extraction, Click Quality support ticket template, credit appeal checklist

Automate Invalid Clicks Report Extraction (GAS/BigQuery)
Schedule daily export of Invalid Clicks report (Campaign → Click Quality → Invalid Clicks) to BigQuery/Sheets. Track: Campaign, Date, Invalid Clicks, Invalid Click Rate, Estimated Refund.
Google Ads High Priority
Document Click Quality Support Ticket Template
Standardize ticket: Account CID, Date Range, Campaigns Affected, Invalid Click Rate %, Estimated Wasted Spend, Screenshots of Click Quality Report, Requested Credit Amount.
Google Ads High Priority
File Monthly Credit Appeals (Before 15th)
Google processes credits monthly. Submit by 15th for prior month. Include: CID, date range, campaigns, invalid click %, calculated refund. Reference prior approved tickets.
Google Ads High Priority
Track Meta Ads Quality Refund Requests
Meta doesn't auto-refund. File via Business Support: "Invalid Traffic / Click Fraud" category. Provide: Campaign IDs, date range, evidence (honeypot fills, datacenter IPs), requested amount.
Meta Ads Medium Priority
Document TikTok Traffic Quality Disputes
TikTok requires: Campaign ID, ttclid samples, IP/ASN evidence, timestamp logs. File via TikTok Ads Manager → Support → Traffic Quality. Response SLA: 5–10 business days.
TikTok Medium Priority
Maintain Clawback Ledger (Quarterly Reconciliation)
Spreadsheet: Platform, Month, Claimed $, Approved $, Denied $, Pending $, Ticket IDs. Target: recover > 80% of documented invalid spend. Escalate denials with fresh evidence.
All Platforms High Priority
